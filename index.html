About Me

Mikey Fabroa — the person behind the pipelines.

I'm Mikey Fabroa — a Marketing AI Automation Specialist and Certified GoHighLevel Builder based in Metro Manila, Philippines. Before automation became my full-time focus, I spent three years in bookkeeping and virtual assistant work, which is exactly why my builds don't just move leads around — they respect the financial and operational reality behind them.

Today I design and build the GoHighLevel, n8n, Zapier, and Make systems that handle lead capture, follow-up, booking, and reporting for service businesses — so the people running them can spend their time on the parts of the job only a human can do.

Got A Question?

Frequently asked, honestly answered.

What platforms do you build in?

Mostly GoHighLevel for CRM and pipelines, with n8n, Zapier, and Make handling the automation layer between your tools — email, SMS, calendars, spreadsheets, and AI agents.

How long does a typical build take?

Most single-pipeline builds (like the case studies above) run 1–3 weeks depending on complexity and how many systems need to be connected.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes — every build ships with a handover walkthrough, and ongoing support/maintenance is available month to month.

What industries do you work with?

Service-based businesses mostly — clinics, home services, personal care, and coaching — anywhere leads come in faster than a team can manually follow up.

How much does an automation system cost?

It depends entirely on scope — book a discovery call and I'll give you a clear number after mapping your process, not before.
