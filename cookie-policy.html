1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They're commonly used to make a site function, remember preferences, or, on many sites, to track behavior for analytics and advertising. Related technologies (localStorage, embedded iframes) can behave similarly.

2. How This Site Actually Uses Cookies

3. Third-Party Cookies & Requests

  • GoHighLevel / LeadConnector (booking widget, embedded at links.levelupservices.cc) — loaded as an iframe so you can pick a time without leaving the Site. As a separate origin, it may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy; we don't control or read those cookies.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — serves the Site's typefaces. Google's font-serving CDN does not set tracking cookies, but the request does reach Google's servers and includes your IP address.

4. Managing Your Preferences

On your first visit, a banner lets you choose "Accept All" or "Necessary Only." You can change your mind at any time by clearing this Site's data in your browser settings (which resets the banner) or by adjusting cookie permissions directly in your browser. Most browsers also let you block third-party cookies entirely, which may affect the booking widget's functionality.
